A small window into the system / 01

One day. More than a list.

A birthday, a task, a trip taking shape. Start with what matters now; follow it into everything around it.

WORKING PROTOTYPESAMPLE DATASTREAM ↔ LIBRARY
A / THE DAY

Start with what needs your attention.

A birthday coming up. A task you can finally act on. Something new in a topic you follow. Open an item and the relevant notes, people and plans are right there.

B / THE WHOLE

Find the whole story.

Browse everything you keep as a table, a collection of documents or a gallery. Follow a connection from a place to a trip, from a photo to a person, from an idea to its source. The same information, wherever you need it.

The connections are the point / 02

A trip is more than a task on a list.

It has dates to choose, places you have saved, people coming with you and things you want to remember. In Komok, they belong together.

A connection is a way back.
  1. IN YOUR STREAM

    Choose the dates.

    Booking a place to stay can wait until you know when you are going. The stream brings forward the next useful step.

  2. IN YOUR LIBRARY

    Bring the pieces together.

    A coastal walk, a friend's recommendation, a note from a video. Open the trip to find them together, with links back to where they came from.

  3. AFTER YOU GET HOME

    The plan becomes a memory.

    Your photos join the places, people and days of the trip. You can find them again from any of those connections.

A growing body of knowledge / 03

Keep the idea.
Keep the source.

An article, a video or a podcast can add to what you already know. Komok is being built to find the useful ideas, connect them to your notes and show what is new. You can correct the result and return to the original passage.

A HELPER, NOT ANOTHER ISLAND

Help with the organising.

Ask which saved places belong on your trip, or turn a goal into a plan. An assistant can help you organise the information and create useful views, without writing code. Use a local model or a provider you choose.

Your data / 04

A personal archive,
on your terms.

Keep what you need available offline. Build a photo archive your family can return to. Choose exactly what to share, with whom, and when.

01 With you, offline
The plans, notes and other essentials you choose to take with you should work on the plane, too.
02 A home for family memories
A shared archive, with each person able to find their own way through it.
03 Shared only by your choice
A collection for a friend. A page for your blog. You approve the content and who can see it before anything is published.

Komok is taking shape / 05

Built around
everyday life.

Komok starts with real, personal needs: planning a day, remembering an idea, finding a photograph. It is still in development. The prototype is a way to explore how these things can work together.

Heapy is the Kotlin ecosystem growing around Komok: useful products, and reusable libraries built along the way.
